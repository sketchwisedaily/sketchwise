Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains how the Sketchwise uploader application ("the app") handles information. The app is a private tool used only by the owner of the Sketchwise YouTube channel (@Sketchwisedaily) to upload the channel's own videos.

What the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It requests only the permission needed to upload videos (youtube.upload). It does not read, collect or store any data about viewers or other YouTube users.

How data is stored

The sign-in token that lets the app upload videos is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sent to or shared with any third party. Video files and their titles and descriptions are created by Sketchwise and are sent only to YouTube.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any personal data.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
